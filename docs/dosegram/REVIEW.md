# Dosegram document set: cross-document review and dispositions

Status: record of a review, prepared 2026-11-12 and finalised 2026-11-13. It is part of the proposal in this directory and is not in force until the owner approves the plan ([`README.md`](README.md)).

## 1. What was reviewed and how

The six documents of this directory (PRODUCT_SPEC, BOT_UX, MINI_APP, ARCHITECTURE, VISUALS_AND_ANALYTICS, ROADMAP) were written in parallel and did not read each other. A review through three lenses followed:

| Lens | Question it asks |
|---|---|
| feasibility | Can this be built on the Telegram platform as specified? |
| consistency | Do two documents say different things about the same behaviour, name or number? |
| delivery-risk | Would the roadmap, followed literally, ship something unsafe, untested or out of order? |

The review raised 64 findings: 5 blockers, 33 major and 26 minor. This file lists each one with its disposition. The dispositions use three words:

| Disposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Fixed | The affected documents were edited so that they agree. The column names the places. |
| Rejected | The finding, or one part of it, was not applied. The reason is given. |
| Deferred to owner | The finding needs a decision that only the owner can make. The documents carry the recommended answer provisionally, and the question is listed in ROADMAP section 10 and in README section 5. |

Where a finding produced a cross-document conflict, the conflict has a row in ROADMAP section 1.9 (X-23 to X-39), which is the place slice G0.1 works from.

## 2. Limits of this record

1. The fixes are targeted edits. No document was re-read from start to end after its edits. Each amended document says so in its "not checked" section, and ROADMAP slice G0.1 verifies every row of section 1.9 with a scripted search.
2. The edits were made in two passes. The first pass amended the five design documents and ROADMAP sections 0 to 3.6. The second pass amended ROADMAP slices G5 to G12, sections 4 to 13 and both appendices, corrected a small number of values the first pass had missed, and wrote this file. The second pass checked the first by searching for the retired values, not by reading every amended passage.
3. Platform statements marked VERIFIED in the findings were verified by the reviewers against the cited Telegram pages on 2026-11-12. They were not fetched again when the documents were finalised.
4. Known residue, left for G0.1 on purpose because it is non-normative or large:
   - the state sketches of VISUALS section 4.5 still carry the withdrawn mood names ("Rough", "Drained");
   - the transcripts of BOT_UX section 21 and the strings of BOT_UX section 22.2 were not rewritten; where they differ from an amended requirement, the requirement wins;
   - the rows X-02 to X-05, X-07, X-09, X-11 and X-13 to X-22 of ROADMAP section 1.9 predate the review and are still to be applied (for example BOT_UX decision D2 still describes a pinned Today card, against X-20);
   - ARCHITECTURE section 13.3 keeps the withdrawn model-based meal estimate as a record of a rejected design, under a status note;
   - the traceability matrix (`docs/traceability.csv`) does not exist yet; finding 27 defines it and G0.1 produces it;
   - the two graphs of ROADMAP section 2 were not redrawn; they show milestones only and remain correct at that level.
