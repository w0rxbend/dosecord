# ADR-024: Tracking entries, rollups and honest statistics

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

Dosegram adds domains next to medication: hydration and mood first, then sleep and meals. Each domain records what the user entered and shows counts over those records. Three things had to be fixed before the second domain was written, because every later domain copies them (ROADMAP section 5.1, the domain template).

1. How an entry is stored, so that a correction stays visible and "the user's day" never moves afterwards.
2. How figures for a period are computed, so that an image, a caption, the text view and the Mini App show the same number.
3. How a figure with too little data is kept from being shown as a number.

ARCHITECTURE sections 10.1, 10.6, 10.8 and 11 give the design. VISUALS_AND_ANALYTICS sections 7 and 8 own every definition and every threshold. This record states what was built, where it differs from those sections, and why.

## Decision

### Entries

A tracking entry is an immutable row. It carries `occurred_at`, the `local_date` and `tz` in force when it was made, `source`, a `client_key` that is unique per account, and `created_at`. A stored entry accepts one change, its first void (`voided_at` with `void_reason`: `undone`, `deleted` or `superseded`). A correction is a new row that names the row it replaces in `supersedes_id`. The database enforces this with a trigger; a delete needs the erasure role, as for `dose_actions`.

The client key is the idempotency key of the creating request or the callback slot id. One tap, or one retried request, records once: the insert is `ON CONFLICT (account_id, client_key) DO NOTHING`, and the use case answers a repeat with the entry that exists.

An entry "for yesterday" has no time of its own. It is stored at the last second of that local day, so its instant agrees with its local date.

### Rollups

`daily_rollups` holds one row per account, local day and domain. A row is a pure function of the source rows of its key. It is recomputed in full, never incremented, and holds counts and sums only: no name, no note, no mean.

`rollup_dirty` is the work queue. A write to a source table queues the affected days in the same transaction, by a database trigger:

| Source | Days queued |
|---|---|
| `hydration_entries`, `mood_checkins` | The `local_date` of the row |
| `hydration_goals` | Every recorded day from the first day of the change, because the rollup carries the goal in force on its day |
| `dose_occurrences`, on insert and on a change of outcome: a status other than a move between the open statuses, an outcome instant, the scheduled instant, or the due window of a taken dose | The UTC date of each scheduled, effective, skip and miss instant, and the day before and after it |
| `users.timezone` | Every medication day of the account |

Three paths consume the queue and end in the same rows: a worker step (`RollupService.drain`), the inline refresh of a read (`RollupService.refresh`, ARCHITECTURE INS-03), and the rebuild of a range (`RollupService.rebuild`, INS-04). A claim deletes the queue rows it takes under `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`. If the transaction fails, the keys are back in the queue.

### Honest statistics

A derived figure is a `Stat[A]`: `Shown(value, n, window, basis)`, `Insufficient(n, required, window)` or `NoVariation(n, window)`. The constructor of `Shown` and the gate that builds it are private to `core.domain.insights`. The minimum samples are a closed list (`MinimumSample`) taken from VISUALS sections 7.1, 7.2, 7.6 and 8.5; a statistic names its minimum and never passes a number of its own. Raw counts ("3 of 4 taken") are not derived figures and stay plain numbers.

A comparison between two domains is a `Comparison`: `Shown`, `TooFewDays`, `TooFewInGroup`, `NoVariation` or `Withheld`. It runs the procedure of VISUALS section 8.5 over a closed rule registry. The first rule is IR-04: mood on the days the user's own water goal was reached, against the other recorded days. No rule reads a medication measure, and a test over the registry fails if one is added.

Text for statistics and comparisons is built from catalogue templates. A lint (`InsightPhrases`) rejects causal, evaluative, advisory, statistical and clinical wording in the statistics, insight and mood namespaces.

## Deviations from the specification, and reasons

| Topic | Specification | Built | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Migration | ROADMAP section 0.5: a domain table arrives by a versioned migration after the baseline freeze | The tables are in `V1__baseline.sql` | The owner authorised editing the baseline in place for this work; no production data exists |
| Dirty marker | ARCHITECTURE INS-02: "a write marks the key dirty in its own transaction" | A database trigger marks it | The dose tables are written by the reminder loop, the intake handlers and the schedule lifecycle. A trigger covers all of them without a change to the engine, and no later write path can forget it |
| Mood rollup | ARCHITECTURE section 11.1: `{n, valenceMean, valenceMin, valenceMax, energyMean}` | `{n, valenceSum, valenceMin, valenceMax, energyN, energySum}` | Sums are exact, so a rebuilt rollup equals the first one bit for bit, and energy keeps its own count: a check-in without energy is absent, not the middle step (PRODUCT_SPEC R-MOO-2) |
| Medication rollup | ARCHITECTURE section 11.1: one flat object `{scheduled, takenOnTime, ...}` | `{perMedication: [{medicationId, takenOnTime, takenLate, skipped, missed, unknown, open, asNeeded}]}` | The per-medication figures of BOT_UX section 12.5 and of the adherence strip need the split. `scheduled` is the sum of the buckets and is not stored |
| Hydration goal | ARCHITECTURE section 10.6: `target_ml int CHECK (...)` | `target_ml` may be NULL | PRODUCT_SPEC R-HYD-2: a user may have no goal. NULL from a day on means "no goal from that day" |
| Transaction handle | ARCHITECTURE DEP-10: one `Tx` with a group per module | A separate `TrackingUnitOfWork` with `TrackingTx` | The repository groups are ROADMAP slice G2.1, which is not built. Widening the flat `Tx` would have changed five in-memory doubles in three modules while other tracks were adding their own. G2.1 folds both handles into one |
| Streak | The task text of this work asked for a streak with grace | Not built | VISUALS section 7.3 and ROADMAP conflict X-24 withdraw every run of consecutive days, and PRODUCT_SPEC N11 forbids a counter on medication intake. The inherited `Adherence.streak` stays unused |
| First comparison | The task text gave "mood versus adherence" as an example | IR-04, water goal and mood | VISUALS section 8.3 and PRODUCT_SPEC R-INS-9 forbid a medication measure on either side of a comparison |

## Addendum, 2026-11-13: one transaction handle

The deviation "Transaction handle" above is closed. `TrackingUnitOfWork`, `TrackingTx` and `PgTrackingUnitOfWork` are removed. `Tx` exposes the tracking repositories as the group `tracking` and the consent ledger as `consents`, both bound to the connection of the transaction (ROADMAP G2.1, ARCHITECTURE DEP-10). `HydrationService`, `MoodService` and `StatsQueryService` take the `Tx` of their caller as their first argument and open no transaction; `RollupService` opens one only for the worker step and the rebuild. Consequences: an entry and a consent commit or roll back with the inbound event that caused them, and a capture event holds one pooled connection (`CaptureTransactionPgSuite` records twelve simultaneous events on a pool of two). The rollup queue is drained by a worker fork of `app`; a read still refreshes the days of its own range.

## Not decided here

The consent ledger (ROADMAP G2.8): the use cases ask a `ConsentCheck` port, which has no database implementation yet. Row-level security and encryption of content columns (G2.3, G2.4): the new tables carry `account_id` and the `_enc` columns, and have no policy and no key yet. The tag list (`account_tags`) has its table and no repository, because a label is encrypted content. The snapshot table `stats_snapshots` and the insight record `insight_results` (G9.1, G9.3): figures are computed on request. Weekly consistency counts (G9.4). A background loop that calls the worker step from the executable.

## Alternatives

Event sourcing for every domain (a general event store for rows that are corrected perhaps once). Mutable entries (a correction would erase what was first recorded). A generic habit table (quantities, units and goals have fixed meanings that rollups and images depend on). Incremented counters instead of recomputed rollups (a missed or doubled increment is never repaired). Marking dirty keys in every repository method (each new write path is a place to forget it). A streak table (see above). A `Stat` whose value is public (every renderer would have to remember the threshold).

## Consequences

Every occurrence write that changes an outcome also upserts up to twelve rows of `rollup_dirty`; reminder bookkeeping queues nothing. A change of the account zone queues every medication day of the account. A new domain adds its entry table with the two triggers, a case of `RollupMetrics`, and its pure functions in `core.domain.insights`. A change of a metrics shape raises `RollupMetricsJson.Version`; rows of another version are ignored by readers until a rebuild replaces them.
