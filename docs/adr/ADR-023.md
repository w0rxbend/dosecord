# ADR-023: Prompt occurrences, nudge plans and reminder presets

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-11-13

## Context

The owner asked, on 2026-11-13, for water reminders on the live bot, "especially during work hours there must be reminders every 30 minutes", and for presets for every kind of reminder and schedule.

PRODUCT_SPEC section 3.2 and R-SCH-21 to R-SCH-25 fix a notification policy for nudges: a daily budget of 4 across all domains (R-SCH-22, PROPOSED, RQ-9), 20 minutes between budgeted messages, nothing inside quiet hours, a 7-day pause after three ignored nudges (R-SCH-24, V1), and at most one unanswered nudge per domain in the chat (R-SCH-25). R-HYD-10 offers water plans of every 2 or 3 hours. A plan of every 30 minutes over a working day has sixteen slots, four times the default budget. ARCHITECTURE section 8.4 (REM-12 to REM-17) describes prompt occurrences as the second message class beside dose reminders; ROADMAP G5.1 builds them, and no code existed before this record.

## Decision

### Owner decision: the budget is a default that a chosen preset can raise

The daily budget of R-SCH-22 stays the default for every nudge. A nudge plan that the user chooses explicitly, and whose preset is marked with its own cap, is capped by its own slot count instead and does not draw on the shared budget. The water presets "Work hours, every 30 min", "Every hour, 08:00 to 21:00", "Only when I am behind my goal" and a custom interval carry their own cap. "Gentle: 4 a day" and every mood and sleep preset draw on the shared budget.

These rules still apply to every plan, own cap or not:

1. Nothing is sent inside quiet hours; such a slot ends as `suppressed` with `quiet_hours` and is not moved (R-SCH-23).
2. Two prompts of different kinds are at least 20 minutes apart; a crowded slot ends as `suppressed` with `crowded`.
3. A prompt is silent unless the user turned sound on for that kind (REM-17). Medication reminders are never counted, delayed or paused by these rules.
4. Replace, do not pile up: when a prompt is sent, the previous open prompt of its kind is deleted, or its queued send is cancelled, so the chat holds at most one open prompt per kind (R-SCH-25). A prompt answered by a tap stays, as the outcome of what the user did.
5. A slot is skipped when the domain already has an entry since the previous slot (or in the three hours before a fixed-time prompt), and every water slot is skipped once the day's goal is reached (R-HYD-10, REM-14).
6. Adaptive quieting, per plan day: after three unanswered prompts in a row only every second slot is sent and the next prompt says so once; after six the kind pauses for the rest of the day and the open prompt is edited to say so once. An entry or a tap ends the run. This replaces the 7-day pause of R-SCH-24 for plans with their own cap, where a 7-day pause would end a 30-minute plan after 90 minutes of meetings.
7. Every plan can be turned off in one tap from `/reminders` and from the prompt itself ("Pause for today"), and every prompt kind can be paused for the account ("Pause 1 h", "Pause 3 h").

### Day profile

An account has a day profile: wake, breakfast, lunch, dinner and bedtime, and work hours (days of the week, start and end; default Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00). The quiet hours are the existing account setting `users.quiet_hours`; an account that never set them gets 22:00 to 07:00 for its prompts (BOT_UX section 14.3). Presets refer to anchors, so a change of the day moves every prompt that uses them. `day_profiles` stores the anchors and `prompts_paused_until`.

### Nudge plans and slots

A plan is one of: an interval inside a window, fixed times, random moments inside a window, weekly, monthly. Times are clock times or an anchor plus an offset. The slots of an interval plan fall one interval after the window opens and strictly before it closes; a window whose close is not after its opening crosses midnight and belongs to the day it opened. Slots are generated in wall-clock time and resolved through ADR-011: a slot in a gap moves forward by the gap, a slot in a fold takes the earlier instant, and two slots that resolve to one instant are one slot. With 09:00 to 18:00 the owner's preset runs from 09:00 to 17:30 every 30 minutes: sixteen slots, 09:30 to 17:00.

`prompt_schedules` holds one plan per prompt kind and account: the preset id, the plan as jsonb, `own_cap`, `enabled`, `sound`, `paused_until` and `active_from`.

### Prompt occurrences

A slot is decided once, at its due time, and `prompt_occurrences` records it in the state the decision gives it: `sent` (later `answered` or `expired`) or `suppressed` with its reason. The natural key `(schedule_id, local_date, slot_key)` makes two loops, or a restart, decide a slot once; the decision, the closing of the previous prompt and the outbox rows commit in one transaction with the plan row locked by `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`.

The `PromptLoop` runs at the reminder poll. Of the slots that fell due since its last pass it decides only the newest, and only while it is fresh (the interval, at most 60 minutes); every older slot is recorded as `suppressed` with `catch_up`, a stale newest one with `stale`. A restart after downtime therefore sends at most one prompt per plan (REM-16). Slots before the plan took its current form are never decided.

`decide` is a pure function over data read at due time, with one test per rule. Prompts go through the existing outbox, the single sender, its token buckets and its priority order with the importance `info`, below reminders. Their payload is the rendered message itself; `PromptOutboxRenderer` lowers it and hands every other kind to the catalogue renderer.

### Preset catalogue

| Kind | Presets | Wired |
|---|---|---|
| Water | Work hours, every 30 min; Every hour, 08:00 to 21:00; Gentle: 4 a day at meal times; Only when behind my goal; custom interval and window; off | Yes |
| Mood | Once in the evening (dinner + 60 min); midday and evening; three moments, 10:00 to 20:00; off | Yes |
| Sleep | 30 min before bedtime; at wake time ("I'm up"); off | Yes |
| Meals | At meal times, only when that meal is not logged; off by default | No: the nutrition domain has no entries yet; the preset and the prompt kind exist |
| Reports | Weekly on Sunday evening; monthly on the 1st | No: no scheduled report exists yet |

Medication schedule presets reuse the existing schedule model and take their times from the anchors when they are applied; a saved schedule keeps clock times. Once in the morning (wake), once in the evening (dinner), twice a day (wake and bedtime), three times with meals, every 8 hours and every 12 hours from wake, weekdays only and once a week (day and time) are fixed-time rules; as needed is the as-needed rule with no reminder. Every other day and days on, days off are named with their rules (`EveryNDays`, `Cycle`) and are not offered, because the engine does not materialise those rules yet (ROADMAP G9.8). The API lists them with a preview and, for the offered ones, as a draft for the schedule editor.

### Chat surface

`/reminders` (hidden, so the listed command set keeps its limit of BUX-CMD-06) shows the hub: each prompt kind with its preset and next time, the medication schedules, work and quiet hours, a button per kind, "Pause 1 h" and "Pause 3 h" or "Resume", and "Day and hours". A kind opens its presets; a preset opens a preview of its next five times; only "Turn on" saves. The day profile is edited by command: `/reminders wake 06:30`, `/reminders work mon-fri 09:00-18:00`, `/reminders quiet 22:00-07:00`, `/reminders water every 45 09:00-17:00`, `/reminders pause 2`, `/reminders resume`. Right after the water consent the water presets are offered as a forced choice with nothing preselected (ROADMAP G5.4).

The Mini App has the same hub on its Reminders screen (MINI_APP MA-SET-10), with the day profile editor and a timeline of tomorrow's reminders across every domain.

A water prompt carries the user's quick-add amounts, "Snooze 1 h" and "Pause for today". A tap records the entry, closes the prompt and edits it to its outcome with the day's total and no buttons; a second tap on the same prompt records nothing.

## Not decided here

- The account setting for the budget (range 0 to 12) and its screen; the constant 4 is used.
- Sound per kind in a settings screen; the column exists and defaults to silent.
- Meal and report prompts.
- Medication presets in the chat's add-medication flow; they reach the Mini App's schedule editor as drafts only.
- Per-domain deletion and export of `day_profiles`, `prompt_schedules` and `prompt_occurrences` (ROADMAP G2.17, section 6.2); the erasure role may delete them.

## Alternatives

- Keep the budget of 4 for every plan and offer only 2- and 3-hour water plans. Rejected by the owner.
- Pending prompt rows materialised ahead, like dose occurrences, with `next_action_at` and epochs (REM-12 as written). Rejected for now: a prompt has no window, no follow-up and no late log, so deciding at due time from the plan needs no stored future and no epoch fencing; the natural key gives the same exactly-once decision. The columns can be added when a prompt needs a deferral (REM-15 defers instead of dropping).
- Edit the previous prompt to an "expired" line instead of deleting it. Rejected inside 48 hours: a line per slot is the pile-up the owner asked to avoid. R-SCH-25 keeps the edit for older messages.

## Consequences

- The owner's preset sends at most sixteen silent prompts per working day, none at the weekend, none in quiet hours, and fewer when he drinks, answers or ignores them.
- Every due slot has a record with its reason, so a day can be explained afterwards.
- ARCHITECTURE section 10.17 gains the area "Day profile, nudge plans and prompts"; `Tx.reminders` is its repository group.
