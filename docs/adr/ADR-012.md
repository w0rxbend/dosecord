# ADR-012: Dose lifecycle semantics — quiet hours, snooze, undo, late logging, chains, unknown vs missed

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-11

## Decision

Quiet hours: deliver = normal; silent = send with the vendor's silent flag and count the reminder; defer = at pending->due inside quiet hours shift due_window_start to quiet_end, extend miss_deadline, append reminder_deferred and set next_action_at = quiet_end, falling back to silent if quiet_end would pass the next occurrence; quiet hours never produce unknown. Snooze: snoozed_until = max(now, due_window_start) + N, bounded by the next occurrence minus one minute, scheduled_for + maxLate, and for chains scheduled_for + interval - 1 min; options past a bound are filtered; miss_deadline := max(miss_deadline, snoozed_until + missAfterSnooze); due_window_end := snoozed_until + onTimeGrace; explicit snoozes bypass defer and use the silent flag inside quiet hours; max_reminders counts the initial reminder. Undo within 15 minutes returns to due with next_action_at = now + repeatEvery, miss_deadline extended, taken_at/skipped_at/snoozed_until cleared, and cascades cancellation to chain children. Taken within late_log_window records effective_at = now (taken_late past due_window_end); beyond it a direct tap becomes the correction flow with an explicit time. Chains create dose k+1 on any terminal transition of dose k and fill chain_not_started rows at day cutoff. At miss time, missed requires delivery evidence (a sent outbox row); without it the row becomes unknown(undelivered|outage). Adherence uses effective_at; delay statistics exclude corrections.

## Alternatives

Revision 1's implicit rules (no defer row, snooze from pending anchored at now, undo restoring a status whose deadline had passed, 72-hour one-tap 'taken' entering delay statistics, chains that stop after a missed anchor, unknown decided by loop heartbeat alone).

## Consequences

Every cell of the (status, event) table has a defined outcome and a test; nightly doses in quiet hours are tracked; a user who presses Undo is not marked missed seconds later; vendor outages do not lower adherence; the UX document is extended with these rules as the resolved answers to its open questions.

## Accepted addendum — UX-00 (issue #2)

No lifecycle rule above is weakened. Original-reminder and detail Taken obey
lateLogWindowMinutes; too-old/skipped records enter explicit Correct without
mutation, cancelled records refuse, already-recorded records replay their
outcome. Three-day-old controls cannot silently become current-time intake.
Manual-log action instances are single-use; Log another creates a new intent,
not a time-window dedupe exception. Uncertain commits reconcile durable state
before replay or same-intent retry. Statistics use the accepted domain oracle:
unknown/cancelled/manual/open excluded from denominator, corrections excluded
from delay, denominator zero is No data. Quiet defer is not outage; missed
still requires delivery evidence. Notifications/navigation/reporting cannot
alter these rules or advise treatment.

## Addendum, 2026-11-28: a dose counts on its scheduled day

Supervisor decision, 2026-11-28 (no human in the loop; the verification of
2026-11-28). "Adherence uses effective_at" decides lateness and the delay
statistics, and stays so: a dose taken after `due_window_end` is taken late,
and a correction's `effective_at` is still left out of the delay samples. It no
longer decides the day. Every dose of a schedule, taken, skipped, missed,
unknown or open, counts on the local day of its `scheduled_for` in the
account's zone, everywhere a day is shown: the daily rollups, the morning
summary, `/today`, `/stats`, the month, the year, the Mini App and the record
summary. An as-needed log, which has no schedule, counts on the day of its
`effective_at` as before.

Why: a late catch-up marked a Monday dose missed on the following Saturday, and
the morning summary's text put it into Saturday ("0 of 2 taken · not confirmed
2") while the day card, which reads the doses by their scheduled day, counted
Saturday's one dose. The text and the card must agree, and a user reads a
missed Monday dose as Monday's. Rejected alternative: moving the card to the
resolution day, which would make a day show doses it never had scheduled.

Consequences: a day never shows more resolved doses than it had scheduled; a
dose scheduled at 23:30 and taken at 00:40 counts on its own day, taken late.
The stored rollups carry `rollup_version` 2; a start queues every row of
another version, and every scheduled day of that account's doses, for
recomputation (`RollupRepository.markStale`), so the figures the live database
holds are rebuilt under this rule.
