# ADR-012: Dose lifecycle semantics — quiet hours, snooze, undo, late logging, chains, unknown vs missed

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-09-11

## Decision

Quiet hours: deliver = normal; silent = send with the vendor's silent flag and count the reminder; defer = at pending->due inside quiet hours shift due_window_start to quiet_end, extend miss_deadline, append reminder_deferred and set next_action_at = quiet_end, falling back to silent if quiet_end would pass the next occurrence; quiet hours never produce unknown. Snooze: snoozed_until = max(now, due_window_start) + N, bounded by the next occurrence minus one minute, scheduled_for + maxLate, and for chains scheduled_for + interval - 1 min; options past a bound are filtered; miss_deadline := max(miss_deadline, snoozed_until + missAfterSnooze); due_window_end := snoozed_until + onTimeGrace; explicit snoozes bypass defer and use the silent flag inside quiet hours; max_reminders counts the initial reminder. Undo within 15 minutes returns to due with next_action_at = now + repeatEvery, miss_deadline extended, taken_at/skipped_at/snoozed_until cleared, and cascades cancellation to chain children. Taken within late_log_window records effective_at = now (taken_late past due_window_end); beyond it a direct tap becomes the correction flow with an explicit time. Chains create dose k+1 on any terminal transition of dose k and fill chain_not_started rows at day cutoff. At miss time, missed requires delivery evidence (a sent outbox row); without it the row becomes unknown(undelivered|outage). Adherence uses effective_at; delay statistics exclude corrections.

## Alternatives

Revision 1's implicit rules (no defer row, snooze from pending anchored at now, undo restoring a status whose deadline had passed, 72-hour one-tap 'taken' entering delay statistics, chains that stop after a missed anchor, unknown decided by loop heartbeat alone).

## Consequences

Every cell of the (status, event) table has a defined outcome and a test; nightly doses in quiet hours are tracked; a user who presses Undo is not marked missed seconds later; vendor outages do not lower adherence; the UX document is extended with these rules as the resolved answers to its open questions.
