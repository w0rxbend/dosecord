# ADR-026: Nutrition data — a meal journal first, with no model-based estimate

Status: Proposed
Date: 2026-11-14

## Context

PRODUCT_SPEC section 8.12 makes food a meal journal, not a calorie counter. The only controlled test found of photo-based calorie estimation showed a mean underestimate of about a third (research brief 03 section 3.3, a secondary report of a conference abstract), and a full counter needs a food database of millions of items. ROADMAP conflict X-10 removed the model-based estimate from every wave. ARCHITECTURE section 13 still records the first design, a `MealEstimator` port backed by a language model and a `FoodCatalogue` port backed by Open Food Facts and FoodData Central, and its summary of this ADR names both.

ROADMAP slice G8.1 writes this record as "journal first, without a model port". This record states what G8.1 to G8.4 build and what they leave out.

## Decision

1. A meal entry is a meal type (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack), an effective instant and optional words of at most 300 characters (R-NUT-1). It is stored in `meals` (migration V4) with the entry shape of ADR-024: immutable, voided once, corrected by a new row that supersedes the old one, with `local_date` and `tz` fixed when it is made. A write queues the `nutrition` rollup of its day by trigger; the rollup holds counts per meal type and nothing else.
2. The words are the user's. They are stored as typed, shown only to their owner, never analysed, never logged, never part of a figure, and never sent anywhere. No code path of the domain calls an external service (AC-NUT-7).
3. No model, port or adapter estimates energy, nutrients or anything else from a photo or from text, in any wave. There is no `MealEstimator` port, no estimate table, no estimate route and no consent purpose for one.
4. No food catalogue is built. Packaged-food lookup by barcode stays deferred behind a licence review and the scanner spike (PRODUCT_SPEC PC-25a); the `FoodCatalogue` port is not created until that work is scheduled.
5. Photos are not part of this wave. A photo sent to the bot is answered with a neutral notice and is neither downloaded nor stored (BOT_UX BUX-FOOD-05). Meal photos arrive with ROADMAP G12.6, behind the `photo_storage` consent.
6. Typed numbers (energy and macronutrients) are not part of this wave (G12.7). The food day card is drawn without the macro bar.
7. The regular-meal view counts the planned main meals that have an entry. The plan is the user's own (`meal_plans`, V4; breakfast, lunch and dinner until the user changes it), and an empty plan is allowed: such a day is a count, never a rate. A planned meal without an entry is "not logged", never "skipped" or "missed" (R-NUT-3).

## Consequences

- The domain carries no accuracy risk of an estimate and no third-party processing of meal content.
- The meal words are class A content held in plain text until the account data keys exist (ROADMAP G2.4, G2.4b), as the medication names are. G2.4b moves them into an encrypted column by a new migration.
- ARCHITECTURE section 13.1 to 13.3 and its summary line of ADR-026 describe a design that is not built; this record is the decision.

## Rejected alternatives

- A calorie counter as the core loop: it presents uncertain numbers as measured and needs a food database.
- An optional model estimate behind a port, off by default: it sends health data to a third party and its accuracy is not known to be fit for any use the product could state honestly.
- A commercial nutrition API: cost and lock-in for a feature that is not the core loop.
